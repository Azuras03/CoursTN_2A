lateral/horizontal thinking skill : different approaches, alternatives

Formal :
- get : obtain/revieve
- extra : additional
- want : would like
- need : require
- talk about : discuss
- get in touch with : contact
- about : regarding/concerning
- make sure : ensure
- give : to provide someone with smth
- tell : inform
- boss : CEO/employer/supervisor/manager
- more : further/greater
- looking for : searching for/seeking
- ask for : request
- work/job : employement/position
- show : demonstrate

Errors :
- No funny mails
- Who she is ? Which job, where she heard about it ?
- No spelling mistakes
- No copy/paste words from the company website
- No mistake in the company name (no contradiction)
- Maybe not too confident (extremely, outstanding)
- Not too familiar
- Not much repetitions
- Not say "negative" things
- Not too strong words
- Not Yours truly. If impersonal, Yours Faithfully
- Not signature
